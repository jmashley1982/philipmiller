    About

    Philip Miller releases original album tracks in the classic style of indie pop electronic music.

    The songs are shared on YouTube, where they haven't yet found the audience they deserve. This site is one fan's attempt to give them a home and point a few more people toward them.

    Not to be confused with the South African composer of the same name, or with Phil Miller, the Canterbury-scene guitarist.
