    About

    “Original album tracks from the classic style of indie pop electronic music, for the online generation and for all.”

    Philip Miller is a UK artist who has been putting music out on YouTube since 2016, with seven albums and a steady run of singles. Most new songs are released about once a month.

    Each video arrives with a little story of its own: a festival-goer chasing a ghost who stole a Glastonbury ticket, a storm at a safari park, an Outrun-style drive through Devon, a child in the age of the Pharaohs learning to fly. Several videos credit flights and drives around Exeter, Dunkeswell and Honiton.

    The songs haven't yet found the audience they deserve. This site is one fan's attempt to point a few more people toward them.
